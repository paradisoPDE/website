About

A small parade through paradise.

paradiso PDE writes songs that feel like the last hour of summer — warm, a little restless, already starting to fade.

Band portrait · placeholder

The band came together over a shared love of jangling guitars, slow tempos, and the kind of choruses you only catch on the third listen. What started as late-night demos in a borrowed living room turned, song by song, into a record.

Our sound is rooted in indie and dream-pop — think shimmer over substance, with melodies that sit a little behind the beat. We write about leaving places, missing people, and the small parades we throw for ourselves to get through the week.

We've spent the past year quietly building a catalogue, playing rooms where the audience is close enough to sing back, and learning how to make records that sound like the rooms we made them in.

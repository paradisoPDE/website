Short bio

paradiso PDE is a four-piece indie band writing slow-burning, sun-bleached songs about loss, longing, and late summer light. Rooted in dream-pop and indie traditions, the band debuted with the single Paradiso in 2025, followed by the EP Late Light, and most recently the single A Small Parade (2026).
